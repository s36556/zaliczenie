Main Locations

Monstand
Mondstadt is the nation of freedom, ruled in spirit by the Anemo Archon, Barbatos. At first glance, it is the most relaxed and self-governed of all the regions, where people are encouraged to pursue their own paths without heavy interference from authority.But Mondstadt’s freedom is not effortless. It is built on the ruins of past oppression, when the tyrant Decarabian ruled the land with absolute control. The modern city exists as a rejection of that history, which explains its deep cultural resistance to tyranny of any kind.The tension in Mondstadt is subtle: freedom creates creativity and peace, but also depends heavily on the goodwill and responsibility of its people. It raises a quiet question—can a society remain stable when almost everything is left to choice?

Liyue
Liyue is the nation of contracts, shaped by the Geo Archon Morax, also known as Rex Lapis. It is a land where commerce, trade, and formal agreements define the structure of society.Unlike Mondstadt’s loose governance, Liyue is built on precision and obligation. Every relationship, from business deals to divine authority, is framed as a contract with clear expectations and consequences. This creates a society that is stable, prosperous, and deeply disciplined.However, this reliance on contracts also makes Liyue rigid. Trust is not purely emotional but formalized, and breaking agreements carries serious moral weight. The central question here is whether a society built on contracts can still accommodate unpredictability and human imperfection.

Inazuma
Inazuma is the nation of eternity, ruled by the Electro Archon, the Raiden Shogun. For a long time, it pursued a vision of absolute stability—freezing society in place to protect it from chaos and loss.This pursuit of eternity led to isolationist policies and strict internal control, most notably the Vision Hunt Decree. Change was treated as a threat rather than a natural part of life.Yet Inazuma’s story reveals the cost of resisting change. Human societies evolve whether or not they are allowed to, and suppressing that evolution creates tension, suffering, and rebellion. Inazuma’s core dilemma is whether eternity is truly preservation—or simply stagnation disguised as safety.

Sumeru
Further west, Sumeru stands as the nation of wisdom. Sumeru is the nation of wisdom, guided by the pursuit of knowledge and intellectual advancement. Its Akademiya represents one of the most powerful scholarly institutions in Teyvat.Yet Sumeru’s focus on knowledge creates its own contradictions. Information is not neutral—it can be controlled, restricted, or misused. The Akademiya’s dominance shows how intellectual systems can become detached from ethical responsibilitySumeru explores the danger of treating knowledge as an absolute good. It asks whether intelligence without morality is truly wisdom, or simply a more efficient form of control.

Fontaine
Fontaine is the nation of justice, where law, theater, and public spectacle are deeply intertwined. Ruled by the Hydro Archon Furina and previously shaped by the Oratrice system, Fontaine treats trials as dramatic events as much as legal procedures.In Fontaine, justice is not only about truth but also about presentation. Cases are judged in public arenas where narrative, emotion, and persuasion matter as much as evidence.This creates a fascinating contradiction: justice strives for objectivity, but human systems inevitably introduce bias and performance. Fontaine’s core question is whether truth can ever exist independently of the stories told about it.

Natlan
Natlan is the nation associated with Pyro, war, and intense passion. Although less fully explored in detail compared to other regions, it is consistently described as a land where struggle and competition are natural parts of life.Unlike societies that view conflict as something to eliminate, Natlan treats it as a source of growth and identity. Strength, endurance, and transformation through challenge are central values.Natlan’s philosophy suggests that life is not defined by avoiding destruction, but by continually proving and reshaping oneself through it.

Snezhnaya
Snezhnaya is the nation of cold and ambition, ruled by the Tsaritsa and associated with the powerful Fatui organization. It is one of the most politically assertive regions in Teyvat.Unlike other nations, Snezhnaya’s guiding principle is less openly idealistic and more strategic. Its actions suggest a long-term goal of challenging or overturning the divine order that governs the world.Its society reflects discipline, sacrifice, and a willingness to endure hardship for a larger objective. Emotional restraint and political calculation often take priority over personal desire, making it both formidable and morally ambiguous.
