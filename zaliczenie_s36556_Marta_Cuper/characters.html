Main Characters

Traveler and Paimon form one of the most central relationships in the entire story. They are not only the player’s main perspective on the world of Teyvat, but also the emotional core that connects the many regions, characters, and story arcs.The Traveler is a mysterious, interdimensional being who arrives in Teyvat searching for their lost sibling. Players can choose either Aether or Lumine as the Traveler’s identity, but regardless of choice, the character serves as a silent protagonist who experiences the world first-hand. The Traveler’s journey is driven by curiosity, responsibility, and a deep personal mission. Unlike many heroes who are tied to one nation or faction, the Traveler exists outside Teyvat’s political and divine systems, which allows them to interact freely with all regions and archons. This outsider perspective is crucial: it makes the Traveler both a participant in and observer of the world’s conflicts.Paimon, on the other hand, is the Traveler’s guide, companion, and constant conversational partner. She first appears as a mysterious being rescued from the sea, quickly becoming inseparable from the Traveler. While she often serves a comedic role—reacting dramatically, talking excessively, and occasionally being teased as “emergency food”—Paimon also plays a vital narrative function. She explains lore, translates complex ideas into simple terms, and keeps the story accessible to the player. Without Paimon, the world of Teyvat would feel much more distant and harder to understand.Their relationship is built on contrast and companionship. The Traveler is usually quiet, serious, and emotionally reserved, while Paimon is expressive, talkative, and energetic. This balance creates a dynamic that keeps the story engaging: Paimon gives voice to questions the player might have, while the Traveler represents action and decision-making. Over time, their bond becomes more than convenience—it becomes a genuine friendship. Despite occasional frustration or teasing, they consistently rely on each other in dangerous situations.From a narrative standpoint, the Traveler and Paimon also serve as anchors for the player’s experience. As new regions like Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, and Natlan unfold, it is always through their eyes that the story is told. Paimon ensures continuity and emotional grounding, while the Traveler provides agency and progression. Together, they make a vast and complex world feel personal.In conclusion, the Traveler and Paimon are not just characters in a game—they are the lens through which the entire world of Teyvat is experienced. Their partnership blends mystery, and emotional depth, forming the backbone of the story
