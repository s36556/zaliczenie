Overall there's way more of them, which is around a 115 (and it's still only playable characters).The original Seven Archons who emerged after the Archon War have mostly been replaced over time.Only Venti and Zhongli remain from the original Seven.In the Fontaine storyline, the situation surrounding Furina and Focalors is more complex than that of the other Archons, involving a separation between the human Furina and the divine Hydro Archon.The Cryo Archon, the Tsaritsa, has not yet appeared directly in the game's main story as of 2026, though she plays a major role through the FatuiThe Archons are among the most powerful and influential beings in the world of Teyvat. They are the seven elemental gods who rule over the seven nations, each representing a different ideal that shapes the culture, government, and beliefs of their people. Although they possess immense power, the Archons are far more than rulers—they are symbols of the philosophies that define their nations.
